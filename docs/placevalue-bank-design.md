## Registers (collision avoidance)

- Teen composition uses the **arrow register** — `17 → 10 + ?`,
  `10 + 4 → ?` — because `? = 10 + 7` / `17 = 10 + ?` belong to numberBonds.
- Judged items are **"Is this right?" + Yes/No** with `display.truth`
  (house rule; never True/False). Numeric judged claims carry the claim in
  `promptText` and the question in `subPrompt`.
- Unit-form drills (`2 tens 5 ones = ?`) and digit drills (`Tens digit of
  47 = ?`) are placeValue-only registers; no other mode uses them.
- Bundle stories use straws/bundles, blocks/rods, beads/full wires,
  stickers/sheets — disjoint from counting's bags and numberBonds' strings.

## Verification (countMath claims)

Every drill carries a `display.counting` claim the QC gate re-derives:
`units` (h·100 + t·10 + o), `digit` (⌊n/place⌋ mod 10), `placeValueOf`
(digit·place), `gap` (next-bundle distance), `sum`, `moreLess`, `set`.
The assembler's `extraProblems` adds judged-truth consistency, word-form
checks (eleven–twenty), expansion-pick evaluation with distractor-collision
checks, and ten-frame/number-line payload asserts.
