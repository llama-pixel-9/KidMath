## Subskills and bands

| Subskill | K-1 | 2-3 | 4-5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| repeatingPattern | AB/ABB cores | ABC/ABCD, positions | AABB, far positions, counts-in-run |
| arithmeticNext | steps 2-5, terms ≤20 | steps 3-9, two-digit | steps 11-29, three-digit |
| geometricNext | doubling/halving ≤20 | ×2/×3, halving chains | ×2/×3/×5, multiply chains |
| missingTerm | mid-gaps + missing-first | same, two-digit | same, three-digit |
| patternRule | state/apply add-rules | + find-the-error, parity | bigger terms |

Band-1 prompts stay ≤20 (hard gate). **String-dedupe across bands matters
here**: a doubling run like "2, 4, 8" can only exist in ONE band's drills —
band 1 got lengths 2 and 4, band 2 got length 3; conceptual (start,factor)
data lists must not overlap across bands either.

## Registers

- Every numeric drill starts **"Pattern:"** — skipCounting owns "Count by
  Ns:" and counting owns bare comma runs.
- Missing terms: "Fill the gap: 4, ?, 12, 16."
- Rules: "Rule: add ? each time." / "Rule: start at 3 and add 4 each time."
- Repeating shapes render via `display.sequence` word lists (circle, square,
  triangle…) exactly like the generator's payloads.
- Judged = "Is this right?" + Yes/No + `display.truth`.
- Story contexts: necklaces/flag strings/tile borders/stamp cards
  (repeating), shells-cards-blocks-books (arithmetic), lily pads/bubbles/
  sprouts/cranes doubling (geometric), smudged house/locker/page/ticket
  numbers (missingTerm), bean plant/brick path/puzzle/scarf logs (rule).

## Verification

Additive numeric payloads ride countMath claims: `next` (incl. negative
steps), `between` (mid-gap), `countBack` (missing first), `gap` (step of a
pair). Everything else carries `display.pattern` claims re-derived by
`authorPatterns.js`: repeat/repeatPos/after/countIn/core (shapes),
geo/geoDiv(terms)/geoApply/geoRule/addOrMult/doubleSaid (multiplicative),
applyRule, slip (broken terms, verified against the shown sequence), fill,
parity, arith, faster.

Note: ~280 advisory `storyWrappedDrill` warnings on application items are
expected — application items ARE stories about sequences; the warn exists to
catch narrated bare drills, and the procedural cells stay unnarrated.
