## Band ladder (mirrors the generator)

- **K-1: no conversion at all.** Same-unit compares/differences, tiny
  iterations, benchmarks, unit sense, join/cut within 20. Every prompt
  number ≤20.
- **2-3: one conversion, larger→smaller** (m→cm, km→m, cm→mm, kg→g, L→mL),
  mixed-to-small composes, cross-unit compares, rounding to tens.
- **4-5: the reverse direction**, unknown amounts, mixed units, two-step
  conversions, rounding to hundreds, near-tie compares.
