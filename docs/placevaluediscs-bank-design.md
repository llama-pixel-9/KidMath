## Registers

- **Visual mat reads** carry the letter-free disc-notation caption
  (`10 10 | 1 1 = ?`, groups high→low, `|` between places; a reversed
  variant lists ones first). Under 6 letters ⇒ passes `isVerbalPrompt`, so
  these serve the numbers-only early levels. `answerType:
  "placeValueDiscs"` + `display.cols` draws the real mat.
- Every prose prompt says **disc(s)/mat**, keeping strings disjoint from
  placeValue's unit form and bundle stories.
- Judged items: "Is this right?" + Yes/No + `display.truth`.
- Application contexts: beans / pages / laps / tokens modeled with discs —
  disjoint from placeValue's straws/rods/wires/sheets.
