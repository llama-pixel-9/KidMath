TURN 5 · THE MEADOW SCENE · 3a WORKED OUT ROUGH SKETCH · NOT FINAL ART 5b AND 5d SUPERSEDED BY TURN 6

The Meadow, worked out

One continuous scene that grows sideways: the clearing you start in widens as the flock does, and each zone extends the same landscape rather than replacing it. Everything below is a rough SVG sketch to fix composition, scale and placement rules for the illustrator — shapes, not art. Seven birds visible per zone at mid-game, per the density you set.

  • Tree left, pond centre-left, open sky right. The right third stays clear because that is where the card sheet lands and where the scene continues into the Pond zone.
  • The Nest hangs in the tree at a fixed spot, always on screen, holding the star balance. It is the only UI drawn as scenery.

  • Motion is constant and slow: clouds drift 26s, canopy and grass sway 6–9s, each bird bobs on its own 4–6s cycle with staggered delays. Nothing loops in sync, nothing flashes.
  • Reduced motion stops all of it — the scene is fully readable still, which is the version the focus-friendly setting ships.
  • Guide handle is 78px and never overlaps a bird: the ground band stops 150px above the frame bottom.

  • Placement is chosen once, when the bird moves in, then saved. A bird is always in the same spot — kids point at the screen and say “that one is mine”.
  • The band comes from the species (wader, percher, flier); the slot within the band is the first free one at least 96px from its neighbours.
  • Scale by band gives depth without any perspective art: 0.7 sky → 1.15 foreground.
  • Zone cap is 7 visible. Bird eight opens the Pond and lands there; if a zone is somehow over its cap, the extra birds fly in and out on a slow cycle rather than standing still.
  • The Nest, the egg and the guide handle are reserved rectangles — placement never picks a slot that overlaps them.

For the illustrator, and open for you

  • Art needed: one landscape per zone at 1024×588 with transparent sky, a canopy and grass layer that can sway independently, plus 22 bird poses at 3 scales. The sketches above fix composition, not style.
  • Growth in 5b is drawn as grass walls receding. The alternative — a fixed-width scene that simply fills up — is cheaper to illustrate. Worth a call before the brief goes out.
  • Seven per zone means the full 22-bird roster spans all four zones with room to spare. If the roster grows, the cap moves to 8 before a fifth zone.
  • Night is a real palette swap. If that doubles the art bill we can tint the day art instead — it looks worse but costs nothing.

Where the nomination lives, end to end

  1. Earned mid-flight — nothing interrupts the round; the flag is set silently.
  2. Announced on the Flight Report — state 2 above, in the level bar's slot.
  3. Carried on the Play card — the mode's card on Home gets a small Sun “Ready to fledge” pill, so it survives leaving the app (this is the one §14 addition the choice implies).
