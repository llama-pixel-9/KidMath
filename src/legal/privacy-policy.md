# PART B — CHILDREN'S PRIVACY (COPPA)

This Part B, read together with Sections 2, 4, and 7, constitutes our online notice of information practices with regard to children under 16 C.F.R. § 312.4(d).

## 6. Our COPPA commitments and parental consent

**6.1 The Service is directed to children.** We therefore apply COPPA protections to every user of the Service.

**6.2 Verifiable parental consent.** **We obtain verifiable parental consent before we collect, use, or disclose personal information from a child.**

When you create your first child profile, we deliver a **Parental Consent Notice** to your email address describing the information we intend to collect, how we will use it, whether and to whom it may be disclosed, and how you may give or revoke consent. **You give your consent by responding to that message.** We then send a second, confirming message telling you what you consented to and how to revoke it.

This is the "email plus additional steps" method of obtaining verifiable parental consent set out at 16 C.F.R. § 312.5(b)(2)(viii). It does not require you to provide a payment card or an identity document.

**6.3 Signing in is not consent, and neither is subscribing.** Signing in with Apple or Google authenticates the account holder; it does not verify that you are a child's parent or guardian. Nor do we treat a device-reported age signal, such as Apple's Declared Age Range, as parental consent — those signals indicate a user's likely age band, not a parent's permission. **Only the process in Section 6.2 constitutes parental consent, and we do not collect a child's personal information until it is complete.**

**6.4 Using the Service without any collection.** The free practice modes on the {{PRODUCT_NAME}} website may be used **without an account and without providing any information about a child.** In that mode, progress is stored on your own device and no personal information reaches us.

**6.5 Consent to use without consent to third-party disclosure.** As required by 16 C.F.R. § 312.5(a)(2), **you may consent to our collection and use of your child's personal information without consenting to disclosure of that information to third parties**, except where a disclosure is integral to the Service. The providers in Section 4.1 that receive children's information — Supabase and Vercel — are integral: the Service cannot store a profile without a database or be delivered without hosting. **We will not disclose your child's personal information to any recipient that is not integral to the Service without first obtaining your separate verifiable parental consent.**

**6.6 No conditioning.** We do not condition a child's participation in any activity on the disclosure of more personal information than is reasonably necessary to participate in that activity.

**6.7 What we do not do with children's information.** We do not use it to serve advertising of any kind; we do not use it for behavioral or contextual advertising; we do not build advertising or marketing profiles; we do not use it to train machine-learning models; we do not disclose it to data brokers; and we do not sell or rent it.
