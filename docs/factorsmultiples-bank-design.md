## Registers & bands

- Integer answers (numberPad) or string choices; the generator's
  multiSelect varieties (listFactors, multiplesInRange, factorPairs) are
  NOT represented in the bank.
- Letter-free forms per band feed the words-off serving path:
  `x5: 5, 10, 15, ?` (multiple sequences) and `12 = 3 x ?` / `12 = ? x 4`
  (missing factors). Seed sampling now prefers non-verbal items per cell
  (buildSeedBank.js change) — an all-verbal seed cell serves nothing
  offline under default settings.
- Bands mirror `targetPool`: K-1 {4..12} (all prompt numbers ≤20),
  2-3 {12..30}, 4-5 {24..60}. Band-1 avoids "nested" lcm/gcf pairs (answer
  equal to an operand) — those state the answer in phrasings without a
  `?` marker.
- Pick drills embed their choice list in the prompt ("Which of 3, 5, 7, 11
  is a factor of 12?") — without it, same-number picks collide on
  promptText.

## Claims

`display.fm` claims re-derived by `authorFactors.js`: factorCount/pairFor/
pairCount (divisibility enforced), nthMult/nextMult, lcmOf/gcfOf, judged
isFactor/isMultiple/nthSaid/pairSaid/primeSaid, classify (prime/composite),
and pick claims (pickFactor/pickNonFactor/pickMultiple/pairPick/primePick)
that verify the answer AND that no other choice satisfies the property.
Misconception traps: sum-instead-of-product pairs (5+7=12 so "factor
pair"), 1-is-prime, evens-can't-be-prime (2), order-swapped pairs count
once.
