## Registers & bands

- Fraction answers are STRINGS ("3/4") on choice items or integers on typed
  items — never non-integer numerics (`fractionalAnswer` hard-fails those),
  so `numberLine`, `symbolSelect`, and `multiSelect` payloads are
  deliberately absent from the bank.
- Denominator bands mirror the generator's `denPool`: K-1 {2,3,4},
  2-3 +{5,6,8}, 4-5 +{10,12}. Band 1 prompts never state a number >20
  (build-time throw in `item()`).
- Judged items are "Is this right?" + Yes/No + `display.truth`.

## Engine change

(`partWholeAreaName`, `fractionOnNumberLine`, `compareSameDenominator`):
they are DOK1 drills, and band 1 otherwise had **no procedural variety** for
partWhole / fractionAsNumber / compareFractions — since the engine consults
the bank by the GENERATED variety's family, those band-1 procedural bank
cells were unreachable (the measurement `compareSameUnit` class of bug).
Parity fixtures regenerated. `selectVariety` was already band-safe.

Level-1 procedural serving verified in-session (bank ids returned, zero
prompts >20). fractionOfSet has no band-1 non-application generator variety,
