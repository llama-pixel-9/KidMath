## Registers & bands

- Procedural drills use the terse bar-reading register ("Whole 12, one
  part 7. The other part = ?") — distinct from every other mode's strings;
  missing-part drills carry the real `display {type:"barPartWhole", whole,
  part}` payload the BarModel widget draws, with the author script
  asserting part < whole and answer = whole − part.
- Band scales mirror the generator: K-1 ≤20, 2-3 ≤100, 4-5 ≤1000.
- Stories are the classic Singapore-style set: join / missing part / three
  parts, difference / more-than / FEWER-than (the trap), times-as-many,
  fair shares, fraction-of, rebuild-the-whole. Bands 2-3 append "Sketch
  the bar if it helps." style tags (which also keep cross-band prompts
  unique).

## Claims

(missing part, fewer-than), `countOn` (more-than), `gap` (difference).

(divisibility enforced), fracOf (den | w enforced), timesOf, fewerOf,
wholeSaid/threeSaid/diffSaid/equalSaid/fracSaid judged forms, eqPick
(which-equation). Conceptual highlights: the fewer-means-subtract-from-
whom trap (`fewerPick`), whose-bar-is-longer modeling (`whichLonger`),
equal-partition audits, half judgments, and bigger-piece fraction
comparisons (½ vs ¼ etc.).
