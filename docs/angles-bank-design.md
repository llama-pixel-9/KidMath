## Registers & bands

- **Band 1 is degree-free** (the generator's design): square corners,
  quarter/half/full turns, "opens less than a square corner". Claims still
  carry the degree math (`divTurn {whole:180, unit:90}` → 2), so every
  band-1 item is machine-verified. Bands 2-3 use degrees.
- Integer answers (numberPad) or word choices (acute/right/obtuse/
  straight, smaller/bigger); the generator's `angle` (protractor) and
  shapeFigure payloads are NOT in the bank.
- Letter-free forms exist only in bands 2-3, with disjoint shapes per
  structure: measureAngle "90 + ? = 180 (deg)", angleSum
  "30 + 45 = ? (deg)", missingAngle "90 - 40 = ? (deg)". Band 1 is
  inherently verbal — like patterns/time, it serves with the word-problems
  toggle on (words-on level-1 procedural verified 30/30 bank-served).
- The generator's band-1 `estimateAngle` ("About how many degrees…
  30, 90 or 150") deliberately shows benchmark degrees with the angle
  widget — pre-existing design, not a magnitude leak.
