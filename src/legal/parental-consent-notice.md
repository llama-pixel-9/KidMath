# Parental Consent Notice

**Effective date:** August 5, 2026 · **Last updated:** August 5, 2026 · **Version:** 2026-08-05

> **Drafting note — remove before publication.** This is the **direct notice to parents** required by 16 C.F.R. § 312.4(c)(1). It is not a substitute for the Privacy Policy; it is the separate notice that must be **delivered to the parent** at the moment we seek consent.
>
> **Delivery:** this text is sent by email when a parent creates their first child profile, and is also published at `/parental-consent` so the Privacy Policy can link to it. The consent method is **"email plus"** under 16 C.F.R. § 312.5(b)(2)(viii) — the parent's reply, followed by a confirmatory message carrying the revocation notice.
>
> **Two things must remain true or this method becomes unavailable:** (1) we must not "disclose" children's personal information as § 312.2 defines it — service providers supplying support for internal operations are carved out, so Supabase and Vercel are fine, but **any advertising SDK, marketing pixel, or analytics tool that receives children's data ends our eligibility for this method immediately**; and (2) the confirmatory message must carry the revocation notice, which § 312.5(b)(2)(viii) requires in terms.
>
> The seven statutory elements of § 312.4(c)(1) are mapped in the comment block in `src/legal/index.js`. Do not edit the substance without checking each one still appears.

## About this notice

You are receiving this notice because you have started to create a child profile on **{{PRODUCT_NAME}}**, operated by {{ENTITY_NAME}}.

{{PRODUCT_NAME}} is an educational platform directed to children. Under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (**COPPA**), we must obtain your verifiable consent as a parent or legal guardian **before** we collect, use, or disclose personal information from your child. This notice tells you what we intend to collect and how you can give or withhold that consent.

**We collected your email address for the sole purpose of obtaining your consent.** We have not yet collected any personal information from your child, and we will not do so until you consent.

**Your consent is required** for the collection, use, or disclosure of your child's personal information.

---

## 1. What we intend to collect from your child

| Information | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| **First name** (a nickname or initial is fine — we do not verify it) | You enter it |
| **Age** | You enter it |
| **Grade level** | You enter it |
| **A profile identifier** — a random code that links practice records to the right profile | Generated by us |
| **Practice activity** — which questions were presented, which answers were given, whether they were correct, levels reached, items queued for review, and stars, stickers, streaks, and badges earned | Recorded as your child practises |

**We do not collect, and there is nowhere in {{PRODUCT_NAME}} to enter:** your child's last name, date of birth, home address, telephone number, email address, photograph, voice recording, location, or any free-text entry. **{{PRODUCT_NAME}} does not allow your child to make any information publicly available**, and it has no chat, messaging, or social features of any kind.

---

## 2. How we intend to use it

We use your child's information only to:

- show your child their own profile;
- set an appropriate starting difficulty and align practice to their grade;
- operate the adaptive practice engine, which chooses each next question based on previous answers;
- preserve progress so your child can continue on another device; and
- display the stars, stickers, and badges your child has earned.

**We do not use your child's information for advertising or marketing of any kind, to build a profile of your child for any purpose other than choosing practice questions, or to train machine-learning models. We do not sell or rent it.**

---

## 3. Whether and to whom we may disclose it

We disclose your child's information only to the service providers we need in order to operate {{PRODUCT_NAME}}, each under a written agreement restricting its use to providing services to us:

| Recipient | Category | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Supabase Inc.** | Database and authentication hosting | Store and serve profile and progress data |
| **Vercel Inc.** | Application hosting and content delivery | Deliver the website |

**These two providers are the only recipients of your child's information.** Our payment providers (Stripe and Apple) receive information about **you** in connection with billing; they do not receive your child's information. We do not disclose your child's information to advertising networks, analytics companies, or data brokers.

### Your right to consent without agreeing to third-party disclosure

**You may consent to our collection and use of your child's personal information without consenting to the disclosure of that information to third parties**, unless a disclosure is integral to providing the service.

The two providers above are integral: {{PRODUCT_NAME}} cannot store a profile without a database, and cannot be delivered without hosting. **If we ever wish to disclose your child's information to any other recipient, we will ask you separately first, and you may decline while continuing to use {{PRODUCT_NAME}}.**

## 4. How to give your consent

You will not need a payment card or an identity document.

1. **Reply to this message with the word CONSENT**, or select the confirmation button in it.
2. **We send you a second, confirming message** telling you what you agreed to and how to revoke it. Keep it — it is your record.

This is the "email plus additional steps" method of obtaining verifiable parental consent permitted by **16 C.F.R. § 312.5(b)(2)(viii)**.

By consenting you confirm that **you are the parent or legal guardian** of the child whose profile you are creating.

**If the confirmation is not expected.** If you receive a confirming message and you did not just give consent, someone else may have used your account. **Follow the revocation link in that message immediately** — we will delete the profile and everything associated with it, and stop collecting.

---

## 5. If you do not consent

You do not have to consent. If you would prefer not to:

- **Simply do not reply.** If you do not consent within a reasonable time, **we will delete your and your child's online contact information and names from our records.**
- Your child can still use the **free practice modes on the {{PRODUCT_NAME}} website with no account at all.** In that mode, progress stays on your own device and no information about your child reaches us.

---

## 6. How to withdraw consent later, and your other rights

At any time, at no cost, you may:

- **Review** all personal information we have collected from your child — sign in and open your account area, or write to {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}};
- **Delete** your child's profile and all associated data, from within {{PRODUCT_NAME}} on the web or on iOS;
- **Refuse further collection or use** of your child's information — delete the child's profile, follow the revocation link in the confirming message, or write to {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} to withdraw consent. In each case we will delete the information and stop collecting.

We complete deletion requests within 30 days. Our full retention schedule is in Section 7 of the Privacy Policy.
