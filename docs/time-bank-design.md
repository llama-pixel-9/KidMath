## Registers & claims

- Clock visuals: `answerType: "clock"` + `display {type:"clock", hour,
  minute}`; the child types minutes past the hour. Prompt stems must be
  unique per (hour, stem) pair — the minute can't appear in the prompt.
- Elapsed rides countMath `{gap}` on absolute minutes-since-noon-style
  totals, NEVER digit-wise clock subtraction — that misconception is judged
  head-on ("2:40 to 3:25 took 85 minutes — is that right?" No).
- `display.time` claims re-derived by `authorTime.js`: faceRead,
  handsWords, wordsDigital/digitalWords, judgeRead, hourLater,
  endTime/startTime (12-hour wrap), elapsedSaid, crossHour, unit/halfHours,
  monthLen, weekdayHop/monthHop, weekend, weeksBetween, spanSaid,
  unitOrder, dayOrder, minuteHandDir, longest (unique-max check).
- Judged = "Is this right?" Yes/No. Story contexts: swim practice /
  art club / puppet show / garden club, kitchen timers, buses, library due
  dates, countdowns.
